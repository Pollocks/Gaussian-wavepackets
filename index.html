Changing σₖ, k₀, or x₀ resets time to zero. The dashed gray curve is the initial position probability. The right-hand graphs share a manual vertical zoom (1×–6×), initially 1×; horizontal scales stay fixed. The packet does not wrap around.

Gaussian wave packet (normalized; dimensionless units)

φ(k,0) = (2πσₖ²)−1/4 exp[−(k−k₀)²/(4σₖ²)] exp[−ikx₀]

ψ(x,t) = (1/√(2π)) ∫ φ(k,0) exp[i(kx−k²t)] dk

The factor exp[−ikx₀] shifts ψ(x,0) to ψ(x−x₀,0), without changing |φ(k)|².

At t = 0:   σₓ(0) = 1/(2σₖ),   σₓσₖ = 1/2,   ΔxΔp = ℏ/2.

For ω = k²:   ⟨x⟩(t) = x₀ + 2k₀t,   σₓ(t) = √[1/(4σₖ²) + 4σₖ²t²].

The momentum probability density |φ(k,t)|² is constant; the position density broadens because different k components travel at different group velocities. The complex phase of ψ also evolves, so Re ψ is not just a rigid carrier inside a Gaussian.
